About WasteWise AI

We don't just identify waste — we convert waste identification into intelligent action.

Most recycling apps stop at a label. WasteWise AI is built as an intelligence and action platform: a vision model identifies the material, a second model flags fire and chemical hazards, and a rules engine tells people the next safe step — rinse, compost, e-waste drop-off, or hazardous-waste facility.

This demo is a fully working front-end prototype. Classification is simulated in the browser so judges can exercise the full UX without a GPU backend. Look for comments in script.js marked REAL AI HOOK — that is where a CNN classifier and a hazard/smoke detector would plug in.

  • Identify waste from a photo
  • Act with bin-level guidance
  • Recycle (or divert) correctly
  • Improve habits with points, streaks, and impact stats
